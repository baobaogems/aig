## TL;DR cho AIG strategy

1. **Architects** = community ambassador program, KHÔNG phải employee Circle. Built explicitly để "translate mission into local reality" → cơ hội Mod VN seat hợp logic.
2. **Points hiện tại được tự động track inside Gradual** (platform). Manual track cho meetup. → có rule cụ thể em sẽ list dưới.
3. **Tiers + leadership roles** mở khoá khả năng apply việc cụ thể (host meetup, speak event...).
4. **Application process cho leadership role**: review trong 1 tuần. → anh nên apply Mod VN sớm sau khi đạt qualifying activity.
5. **Hosts nhấn mạnh "không phải promotion sake"** — họ muốn ambassador biết surface "real business needs" của local community. Format pitch của anh cho Mod VN nên emphasize VN crypto market gap, không phải "I love Arc".

## Thematic outline by timestamp

### [0:00 – 1:00] Opening
Sam Sealey và Bobbilee Hartman chào hỏi, frame event: giới thiệu platform Arc House + Architects program. Bobbilee nói đã work on this for months.

### [1:00 – 3:00] Agenda overview
Sam list các phần sẽ cover: (1) Arc House là gì, (2) platform walkthrough, (3) spotlight what's coming, (4) Architects program detail. Mention sẽ có Q&A cuối.

### [3:00 – 6:00] Arc House — What it is
Arc House = "home" cho Arc community. Sam walk through platform sections — content, events, discussions, members. Nhấn mạnh việc track conversations để hiểu "who you're talking to".

→ **Hữu ích cho AIG**: profile setup trên Arc House nên có context kỹ về AIG project (Vercel demo, GitHub, AIG voice) để Arc team có thể đánh giá ngay.

### [6:00 – 9:00] Platform navigation walkthrough
Tour chi tiết: search bar, member directory, events tab, forum, regional chapters. Mention sẽ có notification system. Sub-takeaway: hệ thống "keep track of all these things".

→ **AIG action**: kiểm tra profile có đầy đủ chưa, join VN regional chapter (nếu có) hoặc đề xuất tạo nếu chưa.

### [9:00 – 12:00] Architects tiers — At a glance
Bobbilee giới thiệu tier structure. Confirm có **multiple tiers** với points/benefits/roles riêng. Architects tier mà AIG đang target (3500 pts) là 1 trong các tier — chi tiết point cụ thể không nói rõ trong section này.

→ **Verify**: anh kiểm tra `status_AIG.json` → `marketing.arc_house` để đối chiếu point system đang track.

### [12:00 – 15:00] Platform engagement mechanics
Sam giải thích cách hệ thống Gradual track engagement. Forum post được "detailed and as [thorough as possible]" sẽ tăng visibility. Engagement type được track tự động bao gồm: likes, replies, retweets (per Amplification Guide).

→ **AIG action**: Day 9 (10/06) AMA post — schedule trên Arc House forum, không chỉ Telegram. Đầu tư format câu hỏi detail để qualify cho engagement points.

### [15:00 – 18:00] Architects program — Why it exists
Bobbilee: program không phải "promotion for promotion's sake". Mission = "find people who can help translate the mission... into local reality". Meetup program tied to "people who can educate developers and founders and surface real business needs".

→ **CRITICAL cho VN pitch**: framing Mod VN seat application cần emphasize:
- "VN crypto developer market gap" (specific business need)
- "Translation of Arc/CCTP docs to VN" (mission translation)
- "Local meetup organization" (real-world execution)
- KHÔNG framing "I want to promote Arc" — họ không muốn ambassador-as-marketer.

Short quote (em paraphrase ngắn vì critical): "find people who can help translate the mission into local reality"

### [18:00 – 21:00] Points + tier mechanics (Q&A start)
Hỏi: builders/apps có gain points không? Có tracks khác cho dev vs non-tech?
Bobbilee trả lời: hiện tại unified point system, nhưng nhấn rằng "we had... fifty different things we were trying to track and got a little ahead of ourselves". Hệ thống ship được với core auto-tracking trong Gradual, manual cho meetup.

→ **Reading the room**: program còn early — point rules sẽ evolve. Đừng over-optimize cho ruleset hiện tại. Focus contribution chất lượng, point sẽ theo.

### [21:00 – 24:00] Tiers, roles, leadership pathway
Bobbilee giải thích tier progression mở khoá khả năng apply cho **leadership roles** (host meetup, speak event, mentor). Roles là application-gated, không tự unlock dù đủ point.

→ **AIG path**:
1. Hit qualifying point threshold first (Architects tier ~3500)
2. THEN apply cho VN Mod / Meetup organizer role
3. Có thời gian processing nên start application sớm sau khi qualify

### [24:00 – 27:00] Hackathon + builder partnerships
Bobbilee mention hackathon pairing, builder partnerships. Brief.

→ **AIG note**: Stablecoins Commerce Stack Challenge (Day 14 anchor) chính là một dạng hackathon. Submission qualify count cho Architects activity.

### [27:00 – 30:00] Conversations + content quality
Sam nhấn pay "very, very close attention to the conversations" trong community. → Implies Circle team đang quan sát signal/noise ratio của contributors.

→ **AIG action**: comment substantive trên Arc House discussion threads (không chỉ "great post 🙌"). Quality > frequency.

### [30:00 – 33:00] Points system deep dive
Quay lại point mechanics. Confirm Gradual platform auto-tracks majority. Manual cho events/meetups/leadership activities.

### [33:00 – 36:00] Leadership role application process
Bobbilee outline: apply for role → submit event request → **reviewed within 1 week**. Sẽ automate process sau. Events được post lên Arc House regional chapters + main page.

Short quote (process commitment): "We would review that event request within a week."

→ **AIG timing**: nếu anh muốn host VN meetup trong cycle 30-day, plan application 2 tuần trước event date (1w review + 1w promotion).

### [36:00 – 39:00] Closing — Builder community vision
Tóm tắt mission lần nữa: understand builder pain points, surface community needs. Bobbilee + Sam thanks attendees.

### [39:00 – 39:48] Sign-off
Cảm ơn, super excited, see you in the community.
