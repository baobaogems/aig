## TL;DR — 5 điều quan trọng nhất

1. **@Arc viết là `@Arc` hoặc `@arc`** — KHÔNG dùng "ARC", "Arc Chain", "$ARC", "Arc Network", "Arc Blockchain". Token tickers cấm tuyệt đối.
2. **"onchain" (1 từ)** — không phải "on-chain", không phải "on chain". Tránh từ generic "web3" trừ khi context đòi hỏi.
3. **NO ALL CAPS** trong post. NO emoji thừa (max 1). NO rocket 🚀, NO bull 🐂, NO chart up 📈.
4. **Cấm financial language**: yield, APR, ROI, staking, "100x", "lucrative", airdrops, token incentives, investment strategy.
5. **Specifics win** — thay "Arc revolutionizes finance" bằng "Arc delivers 1-second finality with USDC-native gas".

## 1. Voice Circle muốn (cho content amplifiable)

Arc personality (cố định):
- Progressive, future-thinking
- Builder-first
- Direct, confident
- Institutionally credible
- Internet-native nhưng KHÔNG meme-native
- Optimistic nhưng realistic

Tone (per post):
- Brief, punchy
- Clear over clever
- Conversational nhưng không casual
- Minimal emoji (1 tối đa)
- NO hype language, NO trading slang

## 3. Cái Circle CẤM (low amplification probability)

### Financial terminology cấm
profits, gains, returns, high yield, leverage, "lucrative", "100x", investment strategies → vì "Arc is infrastructure, not an investment vehicle"

### Token-centric messaging cấm
token rewards, airdrops, incentives, speculative mechanics. ĐẶC BIỆT: Circle có nói "exploring possibility" của native token trên Arc nhưng GUIDE CẤM Architects discuss potential native token. **Anh không được speculate về ARC token.**

### Marketing-style cấm
ALL CAPS, emoji overload, countdown gimmicks, sales-style urgency. "Arc is sharp. Not loud."

### Partnership claims cấm
"Official partner of Arc", "In collaboration with Circle" → cần Circle approve riêng.
**Dùng thay thế**: "Built on Arc", "Integrated with USDC", "Deploying on Arc testnet".

### Khác
- Disputable superlatives: "the best", "the first", "guaranteed", "maximize", "ensure" → trừ khi có data support
- Negative comparisons với chain khác → Arc compete bằng execution, không phải attack
- NSFW / shock content
- Tease unreleased products
- False/unsupported data

---

## 4. Cái Circle MUỐN (high amplification probability)

### Lead with utility
**Good** (per guide): "We use USDC on Arc to settle payments in under 1 second."
**Bad**: "We're building the future of finance."

### Be specific — show
Screenshots · Code snippets · Demo clips · Measurable impact.

### Active voice
"Builders deploy contracts on Arc" — không phải "Contracts are deployed on Arc".

### Disclose material connections
Nếu có compensation/reimbursement/promotional support từ Arc/Circle → disclose rõ. Tránh vague label `#collab` hay `#thanks` khi cần sponsored disclosure.

### Complete submission for amplification request
- Final copy
- Visuals
- Zero tags (tags làm giảm)
- Links nếu cần (links impact content negatively per guide)

---

## 5. "The Arc Standard" — checklist trước khi đăng

4 câu hỏi guide đặt ra:
1. Does this reinforce trust?
2. Is this builder-first?
3. Is this specific and grounded?
4. Would Circle comfortably amplify this?
